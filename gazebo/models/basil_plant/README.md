# basil_plant: geometric stand-in

A geometric stand-in for Genovese basil: 3 stems, opposite leaf pairs turning 90 deg per node, generated by `gazebo/worlds/build_species_models.py` (rerun it to regenerate exactly). It is **not a botanical reconstruction**: leaf shape, arrangement, size and colour are plausible for a young hydroponic plant, built from parametric leaf surfaces, tubes and spheres, without measured leaf geometry, texture photographs or growth stages.

- `model://basil_plant`, origin at the base of the plant (the net cup's rim), z up
- height 0.288 m, canopy radius 0.083 m (measured from the mesh)
- gz Label system label 2 (species dataset: scripts/tools/lettuce_dataset.py --species)
